RF Alert Thresholds

                                
Controls which anomaly scores trigger alerts and at what severity. Detections below the insert threshold are discarded entirely.

                                

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                    

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                    

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                    

                                


                                
Noise Reduction

                                
Confidence floor filters out low-quality detections before scoring. Higher values reduce noise but may discard valid weak signals. Learning detections threshold controls how long baselines stay in learning mode (scores dampened 50%).

                                

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                    

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                    

                                


                                
RF Auto-Snooze

                                
Automatically snooze RF anomalies that have been seen many times with low scores. Anomalies absorbed into baseline (score <0.4, seen ≥5, status=stable) are auto-resolved by periodic cleanup.

                                

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                    

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                    

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                    

                                


                                
RF Dimension Weights 

                                
Each dimension contributes to the composite anomaly score. Weights should sum to 1.0. Reduce timing_anomaly and rare_class to cut noise from sparse baselines.
